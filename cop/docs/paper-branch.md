this is a simple html5+js game patterned after the classic Nokia "Snake", but with a coprophagic worm of some vaguely parasitic and poop theme. It now has a decent procedural audio score generator and to my surprise, the few people who have played it - even those iniitially dismissive of it - became quickly hooked, I think due to the ovwerall feeling of tension that perfades the humorous and simplistic impression.

Now I'd like to give it a face lift. This branch is going to first implement a GLSL shader using WebGL 2.0 to make everything (at least, everything within the playfield) have a look as if it was made from construction paper.  Since the game ticks and music tempo ticks are syncedf, it will add to the sort of "stop-motion" feel the game already has, if it can look convincingly low-budget animation/puppetry with that cutout construction paper look. We should aim to keep the colors similar to what exist now, and add subtle reaalistic randomness (as opposed to obviously repeating patterns tiling and such) and subtle shadows to give the illusion of layers and small gaps/distances between them.
