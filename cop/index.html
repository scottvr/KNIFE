How To Play

Eat dung, grow larva, hit the goal, survive.
  • Direct mode: arrows/WASD set heading.
  • Steer mode: left/up = turn left, right/down = turn right.
  • Space: start/pause. C: toggle control mode. V: desktop d-pad toggle.
  • Perks auto-trigger when they can save/help you.
